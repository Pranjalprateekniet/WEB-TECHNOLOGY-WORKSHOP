Heading 1 (H1) - Main Title

H1 is the largest heading, used for main page titles and primary headings.

Heading 2 (H2) - Section Title

H2 is used for major section headings and important subsections.

Heading 3 (H3) - Subsection Title

H3 is used for subsection headings within H2 sections.

Heading 4 (H4) - Sub-subsection

H4 is used for smaller subsections and detailed categories.

Heading 5 (H5) - Minor Heading

H5 is used for minor headings and specific details.

Heading 6 (H6) - Smallest Heading

H6 is the smallest heading, used for fine details and specifications.

Heading Hierarchy Example:

1. Introduction

1.1 Overview

1.1.1 Purpose

1.1.1.1 Objectives
1.1.1.1.1 Goals

HTML headings provide structure and hierarchy to web content
